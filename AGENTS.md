Ten workflow amendments were pushed upstream into gdaw and then resynced here: the premortem WHOLE round R0 on any inherited artifact; "measure the move, assert on the computed value"; Step 2f deletion review plus the residual diff; S4 requiring a measured delta or a measured inert-lever finding; the Carry-Forward Ledger; that anti-skip line; the leverage tiebreak when Frontier candidates rank equal; Step 2g, re-measuring every figure the artifact prints at its final state before the stop test; and overruling an objection whose premise a measurement refuses instead of spending a move on it. If a resync reverts any of them, re-apply rather than accept the older text. The tenth: **one subagent per literary reference, not per critic.** Every thinker named in a panel critic, and every text in the LENSES cluster, is instantiated as its own subagent arguing from that author's own doctrine — a two- or three-name critic is two or three agents (`Critic: Apollonian Order (Wölfflin, Gombrich)` convenes Wölfflin and Gombrich separately) — and the panel must still meet the mode's composition rule. No reference may be merged into a composite voice, dropped, or reduced to a label; the run is not compliant while a required critic has never sat on the panel.
