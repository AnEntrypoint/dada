02 / WHAT YOUR AGENT GETS

Five things you get back.
Critics who push back.

Your agent works the way a studio does. It plans the work, makes it, lets critics argue with it, and keeps going until the work holds together. Then you get the work and a record of how it was made.

  1. 01

    Traversal plan

    The order the work is taken in, kept editable. A running list of what to try next, not a fixed checklist.

    ModeAutonomous — the work judged on its own termsNext upopen ideas, revisited and rewritten every roundFirstany unanswered objection, before anything newStarta clear starting point, with one idea that argues against it
  2. 02

    Decision records

    One decision, the reason for it, and what it cost. A changed decision re-opens everything that depends on it.

    Movewhat changed, and the render that shows itMeasurethe number that moved, checked in the rendered pageCostwhat the move took from the workRe-opensevery decision that depended on it
  3. 03

    Critic reports

    Independent critics, arguing from their own literature. Every objection names something observable.

    CriticCheryl D. Miller — one critic, one authorTextBlack Designers: Missing in ActionChargeasks who the record leaves outResolvedfixed, dropped, or argued with evidence
  4. 04

    Anchor ledger

    Every number the work shows, and how to check it.

    TanizakiIn Praise of Shadows — questions the default tasteMeggsMeggs’ History of Graphic Design — checks what came beforeAnthology of Blacknessthe record of Black design the canon owes a place toSamaraMaking and Breaking the Grid — the break, held within limits
  5. 05

    Compliance check

    The checklist a run must pass before it can finish: every objection answered, with the evidence.

    ✓Mode stated, with the reason✓No move quota used as a stopping rule✓Every objection resolved as ADAPT, SCRAP or OVERRULE·Every printed number re-checked before it ships

03 / WHERE IT APPLIES

Identity. Posters.
Interfaces. Motion.

DADA follows the needs of the work, with no fixed number of moves and no preset template: the graph is traversed differently for a poster than for an interface.

A run stops when the whole holds — not when a count runs out, and not before the objections have been answered.

01 Add the skill.

Before you start: Node.js for npx, a terminal, and an agent the skills CLI supports. Run from your project folder.

npx skills add AnEntrypoint/dada -s dada

Installs DADA into your agent’s skills folder and records it in skills-lock.json. Prefer a manual copy? Copy skills/dada yourself. Read setup instructions ↗

02 Bring an ambitious brief.

Start a fresh agent turn and invoke the skill. $dada is the token your agent recognises — keep it at the front of the prompt, then state your brief.

$dada Create an extraordinary artistic webpage for my project. Follow the anchor graph, render the work, invite independent critics and keep refining until the whole composition converges. Make every decision earn its place.
