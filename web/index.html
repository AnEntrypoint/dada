005 / AMBITION NEEDS AN ARGUMENT

Give the work
resistance.

DADA is a design skill your agent can read. It turns a brief into a conversation between ideas, decisions and independent critics. The work gets made, seen, challenged and revised.

01

Follow the ideas.

A living graph connects design rules, artistic stances and counterpoints. Follow a connection. Change direction when the work asks for it.

Rubin→Kandinsky→Visual literacyBreak the grid⇄Know the grid
02

Make a decision.

Give every move an intent. Record the alternatives, costs and dependencies. Make the artifact, then look at what actually happened.

intention→form
03

Invite opposition.

Contrasting critics examine the rendered work independently. Resolve every objection: adapt, scrap, or overrule with a reason.

make⇄question
04

Keep going.

Reopen decisions when their foundations change. Review the whole. Push the boldest move further. Finish when the work converges.

The number of moves is never the finish line.

THE PANEL BEHIND THIS PAGE

Criticism is
a material.

Five independent perspectives challenged these actual compositions. Open a voice to see what it asks.

01 / PROVOCATEURDoes the surprise earn its place?+

Challenge familiar forms. Make the interruption carry meaning. The sculpture's red seam became a physical fold after this critic objected to color alone.

02 / FORMALISTHow does attention move?+

Examine hierarchy, balance and relationships. A shared frame lets the posters' different internal systems remain visible.

03 / SIGNIFICANT FORMDoes the form carry necessity?+

Judge the relationship between masses, openings and directions. Preserve tension that belongs to the work.

04 / INCLUSIONWho can take part?+

Look for barriers. This page gained stronger status contrast, full poster descriptions and verified reduced-motion behavior.

05 / USABILITYCan a visitor act and understand?+

Protect clear actions and truthful feedback. The studio has literal controls, an explicit browser-study label and a real PNG download.

01 Add the skill.

For agents that discover project skills in .agents/skills. Run from your project folder.

git clone https://github.com/AnEntrypoint/dada.git .dada-source
mkdir -p .agents/skills
test ! -e .agents/skills/dada && cp -R .dada-source/skills/dada .agents/skills/dada

Existing DADA installations are preserved. You can also copy skills/dada manually. Read setup instructions ↗
