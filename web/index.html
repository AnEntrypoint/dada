03 / MAKE THE WORK

A decision
becomes form.

The agent makes an actual artifact. Type, scale, color and structure have to carry the idea.

Our starting direction uses a stable grid. The next question: can breaking that grid reveal the act of choosing?

A rendered draft, not a promise of future work.

04 / INDEPENDENT CRITIQUE

Give the work
resistance.

Critics judge the rendered work from contrasting positions. Every objection must name something observable.

The agent adapts, scraps or explicitly argues for the decision. Changes reopen the choices that depend on them.

These observations came from five independent reviews of the actual page.
