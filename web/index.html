02 / WHAT YOUR AGENT GETS

Five artifacts.
Independent critics. No quota.

A skill your agent runs, not a prompt you paste. It plans the work, makes it, convenes critics who argue from their own books, and keeps going until the stop conditions hold — then hands you the artifact and the path that made it.

  1. 01

    Traversal plan

    The order the work is traversed, kept editable. A frontier of what to do next, not a checklist.

    ModeAutonomous — the work judged on its own termsFrontieropen, REOPENED and declined candidates, rewritten every roundNextan open Andon objection first, then a REOPENED moveSeedexact labels, one dotted counterpoint, a living Frontier
  2. 02

    Decision records

    One decision, the reason for it, and what it cost. A changed decision re-opens everything that depends on it.

    Movewhat changed, and the render that shows itMeasurethe number that moved, asserted on the computed valueCostwhat the move took from the workRe-opensevery decision that depended on it
  3. 03

    Critic reports

    Independent critics, arguing from their own literature. Every objection names something observable.

    Seatcrit_erasure_miller — one seat, one authorityTextBlack Designers: Missing in ActionChargeasks who the record omits withResolvedADAPT, SCRAP or OVERRULE, with the measurement
  4. 04

    Anchor ledger

    Every figure the work prints, with how to reproduce it.

    tanizakiIn Praise of Shadows — questions cultural default withmeggsMeggs’ History of Graphic Design — checks precedent withblackAnthAnthology of Blackness — enters the work the canon owes withsamBreakMaking and Breaking the Grid — the BREAK, bounded by MAYA

    Compliance check

    The list the run must satisfy before it may stop: every objection resolved, adapted, or overruled with a measurement.

03 / WHERE IT APPLIES

Identity. Posters.
Interfaces. Motion.

DADA follows the needs of the work, with no fixed number of moves and no preset template: the graph is traversed differently for a poster than for an interface.

A run stops when the whole holds — not when a count runs out, and not before the objections have been answered.

01 Add the skill.

Before you start: git, a terminal, and an agent that reads .agents/skills. Run from your project folder.

git clone https://github.com/AnEntrypoint/dada.git .dada-source
mkdir -p .agents/skills
cp -Rn .dada-source/skills/dada .agents/skills/dada

Existing DADA installations are preserved. You can also copy skills/dada manually. Read setup instructions ↗

02 Bring an ambitious brief.

Start a fresh agent turn and invoke the skill. $dada is the token your agent recognises — keep it at the front of the prompt, then state your brief.

$dada Create an extraordinary artistic webpage for my project. Follow the anchor graph, render the work, invite independent critics and keep refining until the whole composition converges. Make every decision earn its place.
